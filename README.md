Steal every lantern in Emberlight Manor, reach the green exit, and never let
a guard's vision cone find you. Three floors with distinct patrols, a
suspicion meter that gives you a chance to recover, bushes to hide in,
pebbles to throw as distractions, and synthesized footsteps that tell you
where the guards are.

Play it: open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser (or serve `game/` with
any static server). WASD / arrows move, Shift sneaks, click tosses a pebble,
P pauses, R restarts, M mutes.
